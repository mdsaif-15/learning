Fruits are healthy foods rich in vitamins, minerals, fiber, and antioxidants. Apples, bananas, mangoes, oranges, grapes, and berries are delicious fruits.

Fruits help keep our body healthy and strong. They improve digestion, provide energy, strengthen immunity, and protect us from diseases. Eating fresh fruits every day is a good habit.

Fruits come in many colors, shapes, and flavors. Watermelon keeps us hydrated, while oranges provide vitamin C. Regular fruit consumption supports healthy skin, good eyesight, and overall growth.
